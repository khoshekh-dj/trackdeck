The music player for DJs.

For DJs who like to see what they're listening to: the waveform in colour, a live spectrum, and the BPM, key, label and genre from your tags, all on screen while the track plays.

Side A: hear it

A1–A3
A1

RGB waveform

The whole track, coloured by frequency: bass red, mids green, highs blue. Breakdowns, drops and vocals stand out at a glance. Prefer it vivid, or in your accent colour? Both are a setting away.

A2

Live visualiser

A real-time spectrum next to the track info, timed to the sound you hear rather than the audio just decoded. Choose the style, bar density, sensitivity, treble boost, fall speed and colours.

4 styles · 30 or 60 fps · about 7% of one CPU core on Apple Silicon

A3

Plays your files

Gapless playback of the formats DJs actually have. Media keys, headphone buttons and Now Playing work on Mac.

MP3 · AAC/M4A · ALAC · FLAC · WAV · AIFF · Ogg · Opus · WMA · AC3 · WavPack (Windows)

Side B: know it

B1–B3
B1

BPM detection

Tracks without a BPM tag get one: TrackDeck measures the tempo with aubio the first time you play them and remembers it. Anything 15 minutes or longer is treated as a mix and skipped.

Detected tempos are marked with a * (e.g. 126.1*), so you can tell them from your tags.

B2

Key, the DJ way

Click a column header to sort. Keys follow the Camelot wheel, so harmonically compatible tracks land next to each other, whether your tags say 8A, 1m or Am.

1A2A3A4A5A6A7A8A9A10A11A12A
B3

Columns from your tags

Artist, Title, Album, Genre, Key, Label, BPM, Year, Bitrate and Duration. Pick the ones you want and drag them into order. BPM and Year sort as numbers, Duration as time, and empty cells go last.

  • 1st click ascending
  • 2nd click descending
  • 3rd click your original order back
  • Playback follows the order you see
